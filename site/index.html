Notes for Diego · the exhibit, built

The exhibit is this page, on experiai.com/lab/iris, in this order: the reversal, the world, yours (the card). Nothing else. Scrolling is the only navigation; every stage is one screen.

  • Reversal: the eyes draw themselves once, as a clock (month under it). The two lines appear when the year is complete. First visit only; after that the eyes are already open.
  • The world: the 25 eyes as here, plus "find mine" that geolocates (with permission) and lights the nearest.
  • Yours: two town fields with autocomplete (Open-Meteo geocoding, free); the portrait is the pair of eyes plus the card. Own photo = later experiment, not launch: feasible in the browser (MediaPipe face landmarks give the iris centre, radius and the lid contour; the photo never leaves the device), but quality depends on the photo (frontal, lit, eyes open, no glasses glare) and a pasted iris can read as a contact lens. Ship the exhibit without it; prototype it behind a quality gate afterwards.
  • The card: the post. Two places in one year, or one place in two years; any town (geocoding), any year since 1941 (one archive call per eye, cached). Save downloads the PNG (2400×1500); the opened eye saves its own card (the eye as shown + caption). The Web Share API on phones where available.
  • Faces on the page: only the visitor's own, or none. No generated people in the exhibit; the generated faces were for prototyping.
  • Stack: one static page, the canvas code from this preview, no server, no keys. Build: about one week. Cost: $0.
  • Film: optional trailer later, cut from the exhibit's own stages.
